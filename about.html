The Background

From the stock room
to the strategy room.

The Wholly platform wasn't built by someone who read about the grocery industry. It was built by someone who lived it — from the overnight stock shift at 15, to brokering deals into grocery chains in his late twenties, to 20+ years of technology and AI strategy work that gave him the tools to finally fix what he saw.

Ages 15–23 On the floor

Tim's first grocery job was at First Great American at 15 — a summer shift that turned into a decade-long education in how grocery retail actually works from the inside out. He moved to Grand Union at 16, where he rose from stock clerk to night manager and front-end manager over seven years.

He learned the floor: what moves, what margins look like, what gets faced vs. what gets buried, and how pricing decisions flow from the corporate level to the shelf label to the customer's cart — often with very little of that information passing through in any legible form.

First Great American Grand Union — Stock Clerk → Night Manager
Ages 23–26 Sunshine state

After Grand Union, Tim moved to Florida and joined Publix — widely regarded as one of the best-run grocery operations in the country. Night manager work again, this time inside a machine that had refined nearly every operational detail of the grocery experience. He saw what excellence in retail operations looks like when it's executed at scale with genuine customer focus.

Publix — Night Manager Florida
Ages 27–30 The other side of the shelf

At 27, Tim moved into food brokerage — selling product into grocery chains and learning, for the first time, exactly how the vendor side of the shelf worked. What vendors push is not always what families need. What earns shelf space is not always what earns the best value. The incentives are layered, opaque, and rarely aligned with the household making the purchase.

This was the education that made everything else click. He had now seen the full loop: what gets produced, how it gets to the shelf, how it gets priced, and how families decide to buy it — or don't.

Food Broker Vendor Sales into Grocery Chains
Age 30+ The pivot to technology

At 30, Tim pivoted into technology and strategy — and spent the next 20+ years helping organizations optimize their operations using emerging technology, including AI. His clients have ranged from small businesses to enterprise organizations to federal government agencies. The through-line: using rigorous technology to eliminate inefficiency, surface better decisions, and implement systems that scale.

He founded Sentir Consulting as the vehicle for that work. It is the operating practice that has been running since before the Wholly platform existed — and the revenue that funds its development now.

Technology & Strategy Consulting AI Implementation Small Business · Enterprise · Federal Government Founder, Sentir Consulting
2026 The insight becomes a product

After decades in both worlds, the gap was obvious. The tools that help businesses optimize purchasing, constrain by policy, and surface the best deals have never been brought to the household. Grocery stores have sophisticated pricing engines. Families have a paper circular and a gut feeling.

WhollyFare is the bridge. And Sentir Solutions is the platform that scales it across every category of household spending — starting with food, and extending to household goods, pet care, and personal care through the Wholly brand family.

Why Now

The technology finally
matches the vision.

The insight has existed for decades. The tools to act on it at household scale — affordably, in real time, with real store data — have only recently arrived.

AI has reached the point where constraint engines, price intelligence, and dietary filtering can run in real time. On real store data. For real families. At subscription prices that save more than they cost.

Large language models can extract structured item data from grocery store circulars — PDFs, web scrapes, raw promotional text — and normalize it into a usable pricing engine in minutes. Constraint reasoning that would have required a team of dietitians and programmers a decade ago now runs as a software layer on top of that data.

The timing is not an accident. This is a platform that required two things to be true simultaneously: deep operational knowledge of how household retail works, and AI tools capable of running constraint-first optimization at consumer scale. Both are now present. That convergence is what WhollyFare is.

The grocery floor taught him what families actually need — not what retailers want them to buy. The consulting practice taught him what businesses actually need — not what vendors want them to purchase. The Sincere Strategy is the answer to both questions. Sentir Solutions is where those answers live.

His grocery floor experience became WhollyFare and the Wholly platform — constraint-first pricing intelligence for every household. His AI and strategy career became Sentir Consulting — helping business owners increase EBITDA, eliminate repetitive tasks, and upskill the trusted workforce who built the business alongside them. Sentir Solutions is the holding company that makes both possible: the soul, the engine, and the actualization, all under one roof.
