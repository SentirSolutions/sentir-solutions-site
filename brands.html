The Wholly Platform

Four brands. One engine.
Every category of household spending.

Four brands, one founding principle — the Sincere Strategy®. Each brand applies the same constraint engine and radical transparency to a different category of household spending. The engine is built once and runs across all four markets.

Perishables & Meals

WhollyFare®

The meal plan that pays you back.

The flagship brand and proof of concept. WhollyFare builds a weekly dinner plan from your local grocery stores' actual sale circulars, filtered hard for your household's dietary constraints — allergens, celiac, CKD, diabetes, MCAS — with net savings shown honestly, gas costs included. Pilot live in Charlottesville, VA with real households and real receipts, May 2026.

Phase 1: 8-week receipt run producing undeniable Found Money data — the proof point that unlocks Phase 2, the broader portfolio, and the investment thesis.

● Active — Phase 1 Pilot Charlottesville, VA Kroger · Food Lion · Aldi · Harris Teeter

Home Goods & Supplies

WhollyWare™

Buy smarter for every shelf in the house.

Cross-store price optimization for paper towels, laundry detergent, cleaning supplies, and every household staple that most families buy on autopilot — overpaying every week without knowing it. WhollyWare extends the Sincere Strategy engine to non-perishables: no meal planning complexity, same radical transparency, same subscription model.

The simplest vertical to build on the shared platform. High purchase frequency, clear unit-price math, no expiration pressure. After WhollyFare proves the model, WhollyWare is the natural next step.

Pet Care

WhollyPaws™

Because your pets deserve the same honesty you do.

Pets have allergens too. WhollyPaws applies the Sincere Strategy's constraint engine to species, breed, size, age, and ingredient sensitivities — grain-free, chicken-free, breed-appropriate formulas — across PetSmart, Chewy, Petco, and Tractor Supply.

67% of US households own a pet. They are among the highest discretionary overspenders in retail — and the most loyal when a brand earns their trust. A $150B market that has never been served by a transparent, constraint-first pricing tool.

Coming Soon — Phase 3 Chewy · PetSmart · Petco · Tractor Supply

Health & Wellness

WhollyCare™

No compromises when it counts most.

The most complex vertical — and the most loyalty-driven. WhollyCare applies a constraint-first approach to OTC medications, supplements, and personal care products. Fragrance-free, paraben-free, cruelty-free, and ingredient-specific constraints matched against real-time pricing at CVS, Walgreens, Ulta, and Target.

The personal care industry is fragmented, margins are opaque, and consumers are routinely misled. WhollyCare is the transparent alternative — no compromises on health constraints, no sponsored placements, no hidden trade-offs.
