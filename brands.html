The Sincere Strategy®

One founding principle.
Every brand. No exceptions.

01
Zero paid placements
No brand pays to appear in results across any Wholly product. Revenue is subscriptions only.
02
Safety before savings
Health and ingredient constraints run before price comparison — in every product, for every household.
03
Net savings, always
Gas and delivery costs are in the math. Gross savings is not the number that matters.
04
Radical transparency
Every rejection is logged and shown to the user. You see exactly why a product didn't appear.
05
No data sales
Your household data is never sold, shared, or used for targeting. It is yours. Period.
06
Local-first
Plans and comparisons are built from your actual stores' actual prices. Not national averages.
