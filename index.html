The Platform

The Sincere Strategy® engine is built once.
It runs in four markets.

Most household savings tools optimize for the store, not the household. They monetize attention, data, and sponsored placements — every incentive is misaligned with the customer they claim to serve.

Constraint Engine

Health and dietary constraints run before the optimizer. Safety before savings — always. Allergens, celiac, CKD, breed-specific pet needs, fragrance sensitivities: hard rules, never traded for a better margin.

Price Intelligence

Cross-store circular parsing, sale-item detection, unit-price normalization, and real trip-cost math. Net savings — gas included — not the flattering gross number. The number in your pocket, honestly.

Radical Transparency

Every ingredient rejection is logged and shown to the user. Every savings estimate accounts for the cost of getting there. Zero paid placements, zero affiliate fees, zero data sales. Revenue is subscriptions only.

The Moat

The Sincere Strategy®
is structural, not stylistic.

Every principle below is also a business rule. Competitors built on ad revenue, affiliate fees, or data sales cannot adopt these rules without dismantling their own revenue model. The moat is real — and it compounds with every household we serve.

🚫
Zero paid placements. Ever. No brand can pay to appear in a WhollyFare plan, a WhollyWare recommendation, or any Wholly product. Revenue is subscriptions only.
🛡️
Safety before savings, always. The constraint engine runs before the optimizer. A $0.50 saving never overrides a household member's allergen profile or health condition.
📋
Every rejection is logged and shown. If an ingredient was excluded from a plan, the user sees exactly why. Radical transparency is not a feature — it's a founding principle.
⛽
Don't burn gas to save pennies. Trip costs are shown honestly. If a store's savings don't exceed the cost of the trip, we say so — even if it means a lower savings headline.
📍
Local-first, always. Plans are built from your actual stores' actual circulars — not national averages, not generic estimates. Your town, your stores, your prices.
🔒
User data is never sold, shared, or targeted. We know your household's health constraints, dietary needs, and spending habits. That data stays yours. Full stop.
