The Engine

WhollyFare® is live.
This is what that means.

WhollyFare is not a concept. It is a working product. Every Wednesday, Tim pulls the week's sale circulars from Kroger, Food Lion, Aldi, and Harris Teeter — automatically via the Kroger API or by extracting PDFs with Claude Vision. The platform builds a constraint-filtered meal plan from what's actually on sale at your actual stores this week.

The constraint engine runs before the optimizer. Dietary restrictions, allergen profiles, and health conditions are hard rules — never traded for a better deal. What gets excluded is logged and shown to the household. There are no black boxes. That is the Sincere Strategy applied to food.

The output: a five-dinner plan, a per-store shopping list, and a Found Money ledger that shows exactly what you saved — gross savings, minus the cost of the drive. We flag stores where the gas costs more than the savings. We say so, even when it makes the headline number smaller.

This is the proof of concept. Eight weeks of real receipts from a real Charlottesville household. That data is the argument — for the next three Wholly brands, for the platform thesis, for the investors we're building toward. WhollyFare had to work first. It does.

The Principle

The Sincere Strategy® is not
a marketing position.

It is an operating constraint. Every principle below is also a business rule. Competitors built on ad revenue or affiliate fees cannot adopt these rules without dismantling their own revenue model. That asymmetry is structural. It compounds with every household we serve.

Zero Paid Placements. Ever.

Revenue is subscriptions only. No brand can pay to appear in a plan, recommendation, or result — on any Wholly product. If it appears, it earned it on price and suitability alone.

Health Before Savings. Always.

Dietary constraints are absolute hard rules. The constraint engine runs before the optimizer. A $0.50 saving never overrides an allergen profile, a health condition, or a household rule.

Net Savings. Never Gross.

We show you what you actually kept — after gas, after trip costs. We flag stores where the drive costs more than the savings. We say so, even when it makes our headline number smaller.
