Why competitors can't copy this. Affiliates, sponsored placements, and data sales are competitors' business model. Ours is a household paying us a fair subscription because we save them more than we cost. To adopt the Sincere Strategy, a competitor would have to eliminate affiliate revenue, refuse all paid placements, and return user data to the household. That's a business model replacement — not a product update. Their investors won't allow it. The moat is structural and durable.
