What WhollyCare Is and Isn't

We live on the right side of the line.

There's a clear line between patient education and medical advice. WhollyCare lives permanently on the education side — not as a compromise, but as the product philosophy.

WhollyCare Is Not
  • A symptom checker — those tell you what you might have, which terrifies people and is often wrong
  • A diagnosis tool — we are not practicing medicine
  • Ad-supported health content — no pharma banners next to anxiety articles
  • A replacement for your doctor — a preparation tool for the visit
  • A subscription — free means free
  • Affiliated with any insurer, hospital system, or pharmaceutical company
WhollyCare Is
  • Patient education — "here are the questions to ask" is education; "you have X" is medical advice
  • Advocacy-first — "what to say" not "what you have"
  • Evidence-based — every recommendation shows its source
  • Honest about limits — if we don't know, we say so
  • Completely free — no tier above free, no upsell, no data sale
  • On your side — revenue never comes from the institutions you're navigating
The Insurance Denial Problem

They're counting on you not knowing.

Insurance companies deny claims knowing most people never appeal. Of those who do, nearly half win. The information that changes that outcome exists — it's just never been handed to you.

~80%
of denied claims are never appealed — insurers build this into their math
40–60%
of appealed denials are reversed — the system works when you push back
180 days
federal appeal window most people don't know they have (ACA requirement)
