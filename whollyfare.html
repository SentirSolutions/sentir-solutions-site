The Workflow

From sale circular
to dinner table.

01

Circulars come in

Every Wednesday, Tim pulls sale data for Kroger (live API, 239+ items), Food Lion, Aldi, and Harris Teeter (PDF extraction via Claude Vision). What's on sale this week at your actual stores — not national averages, not estimates.

02

Constraints run first

Before a single item reaches the optimizer, the constraint engine filters the full inventory against the household's dietary profile — allergens, diagnoses, lifestyle rules. Hard exclusions. No exceptions. Every rejection is logged.

03

The plan is built

Five dinners, assembled from what passes constraints and is actually on sale this week. Protein variety enforced. Meal rationale shown. The Sunday Buy-Off lets households approve, swap, or skip each meal before anything gets locked.

04

Found Money, honestly

The shopping list breaks down by store. The Found Money Ledger shows gross savings, trip costs (at $0.22/mile), and net savings. If a store's savings don't cover the drive, we flag it and suggest skipping. Gross numbers are never the headline.

The Core

Safety before savings.
Always. Without exception.

The constraint engine is not a filter. It is a precondition. Before the meal planner sees a single sale item, every item in the week's circular has already passed through the household's dietary profile — allergens, health conditions, lifestyle exclusions, member-specific rules.

This is not a toggle the user can switch off to see better deals. It is not a preference. It is a hard wall that runs every time, on every item, before any optimization happens. A family member's tree nut allergy is not traded for a cheaper dinner. A CKD patient's potassium restrictions are not relaxed to hit a savings target.

Every rejection is logged and shown. If chicken breast was excluded because one member has a poultry sensitivity, that is surfaced. If a particular brand was rejected because it contains a listed allergen, the household sees it. Radical transparency is a founding commitment — there are no black boxes in how plans are built.

This is also the architectural decision that makes the next three Wholly brands straightforward to build. The same constraint engine that handles dietary profiles handles ingredient exclusions in WhollyCare, vet diet restrictions in WhollyPaws, and any filter a household can define. The engine is the platform. WhollyFare is its first application.

Cross-Grocer vs. Single-Store

The savings argument is simple: a meal plan built from four stores' sale circulars, filtered for your household, finds deals no single-store shop can match. The cross-grocer optimizer finds the lowest available price for each ingredient across all four stores — net of the trip cost to get there.

HelloFresh charges approximately $142 for five dinners for a family of three. WhollyFare's plan for the same week costs roughly $64 in groceries — using the same stores you already shop, with your own ingredients, from what's actually on sale. The ledger shows the gap, every week.

The Commitment

The Sincere Strategy,
applied to your dinner table.

🚫

Zero paid placements. Ever.

No food brand pays to appear in a WhollyFare plan. No grocer pays to be recommended. If an item appears in your meal plan, it earned its place on price, availability, and suitability — nothing else.

🛡️

Health constraints are hard rules.

Dietary restrictions, allergen profiles, and health conditions are preconditions, not preferences. The constraint engine runs before optimization. A $0.50 saving never overrides a household rule.

👁️

Every rejection is shown.

If an item was excluded from your plan, you see exactly why. The rejection log is part of the product, not a hidden internal record. Radical transparency is a founding commitment, not a feature.

⛽

Net savings. Never gross.

The Found Money Ledger shows gross savings minus trip costs at $0.22/mile. We flag stores where the drive costs more than the savings. We say so. Even when it makes the headline number smaller.

📍

Local-first. Your stores.

Plans are built from your actual stores' actual sale circulars — this week's data, not national averages. Kroger Barracks Road. Food Lion Pantops. Aldi Rio Road. Harris Teeter Barracks Road. Your town.

🔒

Your data stays yours.

WhollyFare knows your household's health constraints and buying patterns. That data belongs to your household. It is never sold, never shared, never used for targeting. Ever.
